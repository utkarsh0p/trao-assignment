Joining Northwind

This page is what we do instead of a careers page. If you are interviewing with us, this is the whole process, and you can hold us to it.

The steps

  1. A conversation, 45 minutes, with someone you would work with. Not a screen.
  2. A pairing session — 90 minutes on a real constraint-solving bug from our backlog, on our codebase, with one of our engineers. You keep the branch.
  3. A planning exercise — you are given a fleet, a set of orders and an hour. We are looking at how you reason about trade-offs when the model cannot satisfy everything.
  4. A conversation about the team with the two founders.

We do not do take-homes. We pay for the pairing session at our contractor rate.

We will always tell you within two working days.
