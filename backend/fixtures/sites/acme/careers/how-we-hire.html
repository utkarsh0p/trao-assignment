How we hire

Four steps, about two weeks end to end.

  1. Intro call — 30 minutes with the hiring manager about your background.
  2. Take-home — a small metering problem, timeboxed at three hours, paid.
  3. System design — 60 minutes discussing how you would build a rating engine. We care about trade-offs and failure modes, not whiteboard syntax.
  4. Team conversation — two engineers, focused on how you work with people, mentoring, and disagreement.

We do not ask algorithm puzzles. We do not do unpaid work.
